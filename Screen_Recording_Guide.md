# Screen Recording Flow & Guide

Follow this simple flow to record a high-quality video for your hackathon submission.

## 1. Preparation
- **Clean your screen:** Hide desktop icons and close any unnecessary background apps or tabs that might contain sensitive information.
- **Turn off notifications:** Enable "Do Not Disturb" or "Focus Assist" on Windows so no messages pop up during the demo.
- **Set up the script:** Open the `Demo_Script.pdf` on a second monitor, print it out, or view it on a tablet/phone so you can read it naturally while keeping it off the recorded screen.
- **Run the project:** Start your local environment (`npm run dev`, `uvicorn`, docker, etc.). Ensure the backend and frontend are communicating smoothly.

## 2. Tool Selection
Choose one of the following tools to record your screen:
- **OBS Studio (Highly Recommended):** Free and professional. You can set it to capture a specific window (like your browser) rather than your whole screen.
- **Windows Snipping Tool:** Built into Windows 11. Press `Win + Shift + S`, click the "Video" icon at the top, select the area to record, and hit Start.
- **Loom:** Great for quick recordings and includes an option to overlay your webcam in a small bubble, which judges often love to see.
- **Xbox Game Bar:** Built into Windows. Press `Win + G` to bring it up. Great for recording a single active window.

## 3. Audio Setup
- **Mic check:** Use a dedicated microphone or a high-quality headset. Avoid the built-in laptop microphone if possible, as it picks up fan noise.
- **Test run:** Record 10 seconds of yourself speaking to test the audio levels and clarity before doing the full take.

## 4. Recording Process
- **Pacing:** Read the script slowly and confidently. The 60-80 words per segment are designed to give you 25-40 seconds to comfortably speak and demonstrate.
- **Matching actions:** Perform the on-screen actions exactly as described in the `[ON SCREEN]` cues while you are speaking the corresponding lines.
- **Handling mistakes:** If you stumble on a word or click the wrong thing, *do not stop the recording*. Pause for 2 seconds, take a breath, and simply start that specific segment over from the beginning. You can trim out the mistake later.

## 5. Editing (Optional)
- Use a simple editor like **Clipchamp** (built into Windows), CapCut, or DaVinci Resolve.
- Trim the dead air at the beginning and the end.
- Cut out any mistakes or retakes you had to do during the recording.
- Export as MP4 (1080p resolution recommended) and upload it to YouTube or directly to your hackathon submission portal.
